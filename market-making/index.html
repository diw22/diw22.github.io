About the simulation

A browser adaptation of my options market-making strategy, which placed third in an Imperial College London Optiver competition.

Pricing
A volume-weighted underlying order book feeds Black–Scholes fair values for a call and a put.
Orders
The strategy posts bids and asks around fair value. Incoming simulated orders fill those quotes, changing cash and inventory.
Risk
Order sizes adapt to confidence, remaining inventory capacity, and portfolio delta.
Simulation assumptions

Prices, liquidity and fills are synthetic, with simplified execution and no fees or queue priority. NVDA is normalised to $100; options use a $100 strike, 7-day tenor, 3% interest and the source’s 3.0 volatility parameter. Tenor is held fixed. Each contract represents one unit. P&L is cash plus inventory marked to model, starting at zero. The price chart shows the last 80 simulated seconds; the P&L line and totals cover the whole session. These are illustrative results, not a replay of competition performance. Delta tolerance is 10, compared with 100 in the source, which favours neutrality; resting fills and price changes can still carry exposure beyond it. The demo clamps quote sizes and suppresses crossed quotes; it does not run Optibook, futures, or arbitrage.
