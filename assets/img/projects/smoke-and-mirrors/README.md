# Smoke and Mirrors — draft imagery

Two original concept images generated with the built-in `image_gen` tool on 2026-09-25. These are atmospheric draft studies, not documentation of a completed physical project. The delivered images were resized and encoded as WebP for the website without visual edits.

## Assets

- `mirror-768.webp` / `mirror-1536.webp`: veiled oval mirror (768 × 512 / 1536 × 1024).
- `veil-768.webp` / `veil-1536.webp`: fabric and reflection study (768 × 512 / 1536 × 1024).

## Final prompts

### Mirror

Create a single original editorial art photograph for a draft website project titled Smoke and Mirrors. Use-case: stylized-concept. Landscape composition 3:2. A thin, frameless, tall oval mirror standing slightly off centre in a vast warm ivory photographic studio, almost disappearing into a veil of translucent pale-blush silk. The fabric is suspended lightly across the mirror and drapes in one long curved fold along the floor, gentle natural movement implied. The mirror reflects an indistinct wash of dusty wine red and pale smoky grey, never a person or a room. Diffuse morning light, luminous foggy edges, faint tactile photographic grain, restrained soft focus, enormous negative space, high-end fashion art-book still life. Warm off-white dominates, occasional muted oxblood only in the reflection. Sophisticated, ethereal, delicate, slightly surreal, quiet. Physical fabric texture and real optical reflection, not shiny technology. Keep all objects well inside the frame with generous space. No text, logos, borders, collage, interface, people, flowers, glowing neon, hard black shadows or conventional glassmorphism. This is an atmospheric placeholder art study, not a product advertisement.

### Veil

Create a single original fine-art editorial photograph for the same pale dreamlike art direction as a website called Smoke and Mirrors. Use-case: stylized-concept. Wide landscape 3:2 composition. An extreme close-up study of a sheer ivory voile curtain folded into a large airy wave, with a very thin softly blurred mirrored plane behind it. A dusty burgundy reflection dissolves through the pale fabric near one edge, while most of the image is warm ivory, pale blush, silver mist, and soft daylight. Photograph the tactile threads and folded edge with one precise area of detail and the rest dissolving into ethereal soft focus. Asymmetric quiet composition with plenty of pale empty space, cinematic fashion editorial, softly overexposed film, delicate feathered shadows, subtle natural grain. Simple and physically plausible but slightly dreamlike. Do not include any text, UI, frames, people, flowers, opaque dark red background, plastic spheres, neon, chrome gadgets or glossy-tech styling.
