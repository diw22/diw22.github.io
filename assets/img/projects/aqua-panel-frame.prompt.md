# Aqua panel frame

Generated with the built-in imagegen tool on 2026-09-26. Original: `aqua-panel-frame.png` (1448 × 1086, alpha). Website export: `aqua-panel-frame.webp`, same resolution with transparency preserved. The frame contains no controls or content. Its image ignores pointer input; the real project card link, preview and HTML title remain functional.

The original monitor asset is retained as `aero-monitor-frame.png`. To restore it, change the playground presentation material to `aero-monitor` and frame to the `backupFrame` path in `data/projects.json`.

## Final prompt

Generate a production-ready transparent PNG UI FRAME asset, a single original Frutiger Aero / early 2000s aqua software window border, not a complete interface. Landscape 4:3 canvas, highest available resolution. Precisely front-on orthographic, straight edges, no perspective. The frame is a rounded rectangular PANEL, NOT a computer monitor: no stand, no speakers, no hardware. Outer silhouette occupies x=3% to 97%, y=3% to 97%. LARGE COMPLETELY TRANSPARENT rounded rectangular inner opening from x=7% to 93%, y=12% to 90%, occupying most of the canvas. Everything outside the frame AND the entire interior window opening MUST have true transparent alpha, not white or checkerboard or tinted glass. Shape inspired by a vintage aqua desktop window: slim cobalt-blue outer edge down the left and upper left, cool glossy translucent aqua plastic, pale icy-blue softly beveled top rail, rounded corners, an elegant shallow raised shoulder or step in the top border near one third of the width. Bottom rail slim and slightly thicker than side rails with a soft sculpted contour. Subtle blue shaded edge along lower right, fine white inset rim around the content opening. Very crisp clean high-resolution edges and restrained specular highlights, smooth luminous aqua material with gentle dimensional shading. Keep the border narrow and minimal to maximise the usable rectangular content area. There must be ABSOLUTELY NO controls: no play icon, pause icon, progress bar, seekbar, volume slider, fake buttons, close/minimise icons, dots, toolbar icons, text, labels, branding, logos, display content, robot, dolphins, fish or illustration. It is ONLY an empty glossy aqua-blue panel frame into which real interactive HTML content will be inserted. No background, no floor, no cast shadow extending far outside the frame. Do not reproduce any existing application design exactly.
